Thinking about:

"Add 6 welders to the Mainline crew for 14 days"

Chat-First Interface

Don't fill out forms. Just tell the AI what you need. "Add a welding crew." "What's my burn rate?" It understands construction context.

Google Sheets Backend

No black-box databases. OpenEstimate writes directly to *your* Google Sheets. You own the data. You can edit it manually anytime.

Local & Secure

Runs on your machine via Docker. Your API keys. Your OAuth tokens. No data is sent to our servers because we don't have any.
