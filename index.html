We're building the first open-source, AI-native estimating platform. No SaaS fees. No data lock-in. Just you, your data, and an intelligent engine that understands construction.

Our Philosophy

Data Sovereignty

Your data should live in formats you own and control. OpenEstimate writes directly to local files and Google Sheets. If we disappear tomorrow, your estimates are still yours.

The End of SaaS Bloat

Estimating software has become bloated, slow, and exorbitantly expensive. We believe in lean, fast, local-first software that respects your machine and your wallet.

AI as a Force Multiplier

We don't want AI to replace estimators. We want to give every estimator a team of junior analysts who never sleep. Chat with your blueprints. Query your unit rates. Estimate faster.

Open Source Core

Trust is earned through transparency. Our core engine is open source. Inspect the code, contribute to it, or fork it and build your own.
